Glimpse emerged from the desire to explore and share the "why" behind human ambitions. Our approach is simple: be genuine, be real, and let the true essence of each story speak for itself.

The determination and creativity of our subjects is not just compelling, but a personal call to push the boundaries of our visual narratives. Each project is an opportunity to explore what it means to chase greatness, encouraging both our team and our audience to pursue their best. By bringing these stories to light, we aim to inspire others to explore their own potential. This is the heart of Glimpse—where every story is an invitation to dream bigger and dig deeper.

—
Founded by Los Angeles-based director Mike Schwartz, Glimpse is his vision brought to life—a platform where we celebrate the depth of human ambition.
