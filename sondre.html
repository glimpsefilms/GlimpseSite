Sondre: The Road to Paris

Dive into the world of Sondre Guttormsen, a pole vaulter among the rare few to have cleared six meters. This film shares a glimpse of his journey to the 2024 Paris Olympics, highlighting his quest to redefine the limits of his sport and to become the best in the world.

Olympian, European Champion, Three time NCAA Champion.
