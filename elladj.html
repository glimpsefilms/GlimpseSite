Wild Ice: Elladj Baldé

An original documentary series in development.

Wild Ice expands beyond a single episode. Following Elladj Baldé across continents and landscapes, the series is a global search for freedom, connection, and identity through movement in the natural world.

Each chapter takes Elladj and his crew somewhere new, somewhere raw, somewhere that demands they listen before they create. In the pilot, Elladj and Paul Zizka venture into the Canadian Rockies in pursuit of something most people will never see — the season's first ice. What unfolds is not just a search for the perfect surface, but a meditation on patience, surrender, and what it means to move through a world that doesn't bend to you. This is not a sports story. It is a human one.

In the next episode, Elladj returns to his roots, venturing to Africa in search of wild ice and the freedom that only comes from moving through the landscape that shaped him.
