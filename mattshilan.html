Matt Shlian: It Starts With a Fold

Artist Matt Shlian invites us into his Ann Arbor studio — a place where geometry dances, experimentation leads the way, and process is everything. With hands guided by intuition and precision, Matt transforms flat sheets of paper into dimensional poetry.

As we follow his meticulous rhythm, we glimpse not just the art, but the mind behind it.
